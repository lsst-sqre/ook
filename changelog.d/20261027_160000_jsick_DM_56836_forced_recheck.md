### New features

- `ook linkcheck-recheck` gains repeatable `--include-status-code <code>` and `--include-status <broken|blocked|failing>` options that force a one-off recheck of the still-referenced URLs whose stored result matches any of them, even when they are not yet due. Run `ook linkcheck-recheck --include-status-code 421` once after deploying this release to re-verify the URLs stored with a 421, and `--include-status blocked` to re-verify `blocked` rows. A URL that is due anyway is enqueued once, and the completion log reports the number of forced rechecks. With neither option, the command enqueues the same URLs as before. Forced URLs are published in recheck messages with a new `force` field, which tells the consumer to recheck them regardless of their schedule; messages without the field are handled as before.
